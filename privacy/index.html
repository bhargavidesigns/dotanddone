DOT & DONE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

The short version

Dot & Done does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you type into the app stays on your phone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app stores

To work, the app saves the following in its own storage on your device:

  • your tasks, their colors and weights
  • the prints made from tasks you complete
  • the words it learns for choosing ink colors
  • your settings, such as the morning reminder time

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Backups

Your phone may include the app's data in its own device backup (for example, Google's Android backup or an iPhone backup), depending on your phone's settings. Those backups are handled by your phone's maker under their privacy policy, not by Dot & Done.

Notifications

If you turn on the morning nudge, the app schedules a reminder on your device. It is created and delivered on the device, and no notification data is sent anywhere.

Sharing prints

When you tap Share Print, the app passes the image to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes from there (Photos, Messages, another app) is your choice and is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Analytics, ads and third parties

Dot & Done has no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking tools and no account system. The app works fully offline.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings › Start over › Clear everything. Or uninstall the app. Either one removes all of your data from the device.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/bhargavidesigns/dotanddone/issues.
